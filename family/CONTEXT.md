# Family

Supports the children's routines: how much they may watch, and what their school day holds.

## Language

**Kids Account**:
The single media account every child watches under. Screen Time belongs to it, not to any one child or player.
_Avoid_: Kid, child profile, user

**Screen Time**:
Time the Kids Account spends playing, counted across every player, shared by all the children.
_Avoid_: Watch time, usage, streaming time

**Daily Limit**:
The most Screen Time the Kids Account may use in one day. When it is used up, playback stops and stays blocked until the next day.
_Avoid_: Quota, allowance, cap

**School Day**:
A weekday within the school year, from the first to the last day of school, that is not a closure.
_Avoid_: School night, weekday, term day

**Special Class**:
The rotating class a child has on a School Day, such as library, PE, art or music. The class is about the day's routine, not the child's identity.
_Avoid_: Specials, enrichment, elective

**Viewing Window**:
The hours of a day in which the Kids Account may play. It follows School Day, not the day of the week.
_Avoid_: Allowed hours, schedule, curfew
