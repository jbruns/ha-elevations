# Lighting

Turns lights on and off with the sun, the hour, and each other.

## Language

**Leader**:
A light or switch whose state another light copies.
_Avoid_: Master, source, primary

**Follower**:
A light that copies its Leader: on and off, and optionally brightness.
_Avoid_: Slave, mirror, secondary

**Exterior Lights**:
Lights outside the home that are on from dusk to dawn, judged by how high the sun is.
_Avoid_: Porch lights, outdoor lights, security lights

**Night Timeout**:
A light left on too long during night hours is turned off.
_Avoid_: Auto-off, sleep timer
