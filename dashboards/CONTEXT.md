# Dashboards

What the household sees at a glance. Displays use the other contexts' language and never redefine it.

## Language

**Display**:
A dashboard the household looks at, such as Our Home or the Wallboard. Nothing depends on a Display, so it can be removed without anything else noticing.
_Avoid_: Screen, UI, panel

**Our Home**:
The household's main Display, opened on phones and computers, with a view per theme of the home.
_Avoid_: Overview, main dashboard, home dashboard

**Wallboard**:
A shared household Display that runs as an appliance on a wall-mounted screen. It shows Attention Items, the Household Schedule, and each child's Chores, and supports quick shared actions.
_Avoid_: Kiosk, tablet, family board

**Seasonal Look**:
The Wallboard's theme and background for the current season. A Holiday Look replaces it for a holiday's date range.
_Avoid_: Skin, wallpaper, monthly theme

**Household Schedule**:
The shared calendar plan shown on the Wallboard, covering the coming seven days starting today. Events have no member ownership unless a source explicitly provides it.
_Avoid_: Family calendar, member schedule, member lanes, weekly calendar

**Attention Item**:
A temporary condition that needs household awareness or action now. It exists only while the condition holds; a normal state such as "doors closed" is never an Attention Item.
_Avoid_: Alert, notification, status

**Responsibility**:
Work assigned to a household member, usually with a due date or time.
_Avoid_: Task, assignment, item

**Chore**:
A recurring Responsibility that may award points when completed.
_Avoid_: Job, task

**After-School Task**:
Work for the children to do after school, not assigned to any one child. After-School Tasks matter only on a School Day.
_Avoid_: Homework, chore, responsibility

**Unlocked Door**:
A locking door into the home that is left unlocked between sunset and sunrise, or while the home is not Household Home (Climate's term). Unlocked in daytime with someone home is normal. A door into the garage counts only while the garage door is open. Whether a door is unlocked is separate from whether it is open, which Climate's Exterior Door covers.
_Avoid_: Unlocked entry, open lock, insecure door

**Finished Cycle**:
A washer or dryer load that has finished and still needs unloading. It is an Attention Item until someone acknowledges it or it ages out. A running appliance is not an Attention Item.
_Avoid_: Done, complete, laundry alert

**Collection Day**:
A day when the hauler collects the household's trash or recycling. The evening before a Collection Day, putting the bins out is an Attention Item.
_Avoid_: Trash day, pickup, garbage night

**Claim**:
A child's statement that they have done a Chore. A Claim awaits a parent's approval before the Chore counts as done or awards points.
_Avoid_: Complete, check off, done

**Countdown**:
The days remaining until an anticipated event: a trip or break, a birthday, or a holiday.
_Avoid_: Timer, upcoming, reminder

**Reminder**:
An unassigned prompt for the household.
_Avoid_: Responsibility, alert

**Shopping Item**:
Something the household intends to acquire. It is not a Responsibility unless a household member takes ownership of it.
_Avoid_: Task, reminder
